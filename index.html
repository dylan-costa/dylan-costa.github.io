Mathematics · University of Connecticut

Dylan Costa

I'm a PhD candidate in Mathematics at the University of Connecticut, advised by Álvaro Lozano-Robledo. I work in arithmetic geometry, studying Galois representations attached to elliptic curves.

I graduated from Ithaca College in 2021 with a BA in Mathematics (Honors) and a BM in Cello Performance. Outside of math, I play cello and perform in a string quartet that puts on yearly chamber concerts in my hometown of Danbury, CT, and I enjoy getting outside and playing board games.
